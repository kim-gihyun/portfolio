# Design

Preserve the personal engineering identity: midnight navy, sky blue, technical paper, a subtle drafting grid, an interactive home page, custom drafting cursor and 3D project models. Do not replace these with a generic minimal project list.

Use Times New Roman / Times for headings, prose and navigation, with Geist Mono for technical annotations. Large names use regular weight and an italic blue surname. Keep copy short and specific without stripping colour, interaction or personality.

The home page centres Gihyun Kim on one line. The project and interest branches reveal automatically 1.2 seconds after the loader finishes. Clicking the name or pressing Escape can close the map; clicking the name can reopen it. After the automatic reveal, leaving the rounded map region contracts it. Touching the name with the pointer immediately reopens it, without a hover delay; clicking and keyboard activation also work. The rounded contraction boundary closely follows the outer destinations. Nearby particles become denser as the pointer approaches. Do not use the gyroid field or giant GK backdrop on the home page. Keep the backdrop on other pages. Cursor effects apply only to fine pointers and respect reduced motion. Keep usable native inputs, focus indicators, dark/light themes and readable contrast.

Ask SHC uses its original Geist sans-serif typography throughout the launcher, compact panel and full-screen workspace; never apply the portfolio Times font to it.

The home reveal has six destinations: Shin Group at the top, CV, Projects, Miscellaneous, Robocon and Laidlaw. CV and Projects start as buttons below the name and soften into full branches during expansion. Their particle endpoints interpolate along the same transition. On expansion the full name shrinks into a softly blurred GK and the description fades. Coloured particles stream outward from the small core; no drawn connecting lines. A blue halo radiates behind the name. The loading screen shows only a centred, softly blurred GK for about 1.5 seconds, then fades away.

Project section buttons have lift and press animations; the individual project selector sits above the model and details. Projects has addressable sections at #laidlaw, #shin-group, #robocon and #other, plus an all-projects view. Laidlaw links throughout the site point to its project section, which includes the poster, conference PowerPoint and existing report.

Ask SHC appears after loading and expands in place. The static portfolio is hosted by GitHub Pages; the helpdesk backend is separate on Cloudflare. Never put secrets in the portfolio. Do not commit, push, or add assistant/co-author attribution; the owner handles commits.

About uses a large opening paragraph and 19-24 px body text beside the portrait. CV uses section headings in a separate left margin and dated entries on the right, without a vertical timeline rail through headings.
