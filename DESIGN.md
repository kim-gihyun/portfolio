# Design

Preserve the personal engineering identity: midnight navy, sky blue, technical paper, a subtle drafting grid, an interactive home page, custom drafting cursor and 3D project models. Do not replace these with a generic minimal project list.

Use Times New Roman / Times for headings, prose and navigation, with Geist Mono for technical annotations. Large names use regular weight and an italic blue surname. Keep copy short and specific without stripping colour, interaction or personality.

The home page centres Gihyun Kim on one line. Hovering or focusing the name, or tapping it on touch screens, reveals coloured project and interest branches. Nearby particles become denser as the pointer approaches. Do not use the gyroid field or giant GK backdrop on the home page. Keep the backdrop on other pages. Cursor effects apply only to fine pointers and respect reduced motion. Keep usable native inputs, focus indicators, dark/light themes and readable contrast.

Ask SHC uses its original Geist sans-serif typography throughout the launcher, compact panel and full-screen workspace; never apply the portfolio Times font to it.

The home reveal has five destinations: Shin Group at the top, Projects, Off-duty, HKU Robocon and Laidlaw. On expansion the full name shrinks into a softly blurred GK, the description and buttons fade, and the five destinations take focus. Coloured particles stream outward from the small core; no drawn connecting lines. A blue halo radiates behind the name. Projects and CV are buttons in the resting state. The loading screen shows only a centred, softly blurred GK for about 1.5 seconds, then fades away.

Ask SHC appears after loading and expands in place. The static portfolio is hosted by GitHub Pages; the helpdesk backend is separate on Cloudflare. Never put secrets in the portfolio. Do not commit, push, or add assistant/co-author attribution; the owner handles commits.
